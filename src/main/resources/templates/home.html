What is GPA?

Grade Point Average (GPA) is the average of grade points earned in all courses within a single semester, weighted by credit hours.

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) / Σ(credits)

What is CGPA?

Cumulative GPA (CGPA) is the overall average of all semester GPAs throughout your academic program. It reflects your overall academic performance.

CGPA = Σ(all semester GPAs) / number of semesters
